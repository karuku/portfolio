02 HOW I WORK

Clarity in the
important places.

Good engineering is less about ceremony and more about making the right behavior obvious, testable, and operable.

A

Boundaries with purpose

Keep business rules independent. Put external systems behind small adapters that can be replaced and tested.

B

Operational by default

Favor useful health checks, structured logs, repeatable builds, and CI that exercises the runtime path.

C

AI with guardrails

Treat model output as untrusted. Bound inputs, version prompts, validate results, and keep human review in the workflow.
